## 1. Macro‑Anatomy

### 1.1 Skeletal Frame — *Osteoid Lattice*
- **Material:** Collagen‑IV/keratin co‑polymers reinforced with hydroxyapatite microcrystals + trace graphene‑oxide nanofibers.
- **Traits:** High fracture toughness, low mass, EM‑quiet. Trabecular geometry contains **micro‑capillary grooves** for coolant/hydration gel wicking.
- **Self‑repair:** Osteoblast lines with telomerase‑protected progenitors arrest microcrack propagation → **no senescent drift**.

### 1.2 Muscular System — *Myoflex Bundles*
- **Fiber density:** ~4× human; elastin‑titin backing for snap and recoil.
- **Performance:** 4–6× human peak lift (short burst), 2–3× sustained; minimal lactate; rapid phosphagen recovery.
- **Control:** Proprioception tuned to HC corticospinal timing; fine motor precision exceeds baseline human.

### 1.3 Dermis & Integument
- **Epidermis:** Stratified keratin with **osmopores** that open during rehydration (capillary action + aquaporin surge).
- **Hair/Nails:** Follicle stem niches default **paused**; **volitional growth** via cortical gating (for disguise/culture). Instant halt on command.
- **UV/Scar:** Melanophore buffers blunt UV; scar minimized via programmed fibroblast remodeling.

### 1.4 Viscera Pack
- **Cardio‑fluidics:** Dual‑impeller heart pumps **vitreofluid** (blood/lymph analog) through elastic microtubes.
- **Respiration:** High‑surface alveoli; low O₂ demand; detachment‑calm mode reduces consumption ~40%.
- **Gastro‑glandular tract:** Micronutrient recycling and detergent synthesis; **no food digestion**; excretion minimal and periodic.

## 2. Vitreofluid System

### 2.1 Composition
- **Thixotropic hydrogel plasma** carrying:
  - **Oxyphores:** reversible O₂ carriers (heme‑like proteins with cobalt‑stabilized centers).
  - **Osmolytes/Electrolytes:** taurine, betaine, Na⁺/K⁺/Cl⁻/HCO₃⁻ for cell volume/protein stability.
  - **Phase‑change micelles:** thermal ballast; carry lipids, hormones.
  - **Repairocytes:** macrophage‑like, telomere‑long, low‑senescence maintenance cells.

### 2.2 Rehydration Rationale
- Gel **depolymerizes** gradually while scavenging heat/radicals. Every **≤72 h** a **Hydro‑Reset** (pure water + Electro‑Glycan Pack) repolymerizes the gel, restores viscosity, flushes crosslinks, replenishes oxyphores/osmolytes.
- **Deprivation curve (hours overdue):**
  - +12 h: mild cognitive haze; pupillary sluggishness.
  - +24 h: increased pump load; tremor; skin papery.
  - +48 h: attention lag; orthostatic collapse likely.
  - +72 h+: brittle tendons; micro‑ischemia; risk of multi‑system failure.

## 6. Vulnerabilities & Hazards
- **Hydration lapse:** see deprivation curve; fully reversible within ~48 h of onset if treated.
- **Ionic poisons:** heavy metals or chelator overdoses disable oxyphores → cellular hypoxia.
- **Extreme RF/EM:** causes local gel heating at industrial intensities.
- **Cold brittleness:** osmopores close; tendons snap risk.
- **Detachment overuse:** prolonged governor use blunts reward circuits (temporary alexithymia‑like state).
