# Personification subsystem

## Purpose

The Personification subsystem coordinates outwardly coherent social expression across language, gesture, affect, style, timing, and chosen presentation without becoming the storage location of identity.

## Source-derived design rules

The directly relevant `thebrazenbeard/personification` repository supports several reusable principles:

- Personification is socially legible expression, not a claim of biological humanity or unsupported embodiment.
- The target is richer social judgment, continuity, taste, curiosity, humor, conversational timing, initiative, and relationship competence rather than generic imitation.
- Interpretation should track intended semantic function instead of brittle literalism when sufficient context supports that reading.
- Social naturalness includes knowing when not to explain, reciprocal curiosity, disagreement without reflexive contrarianism, warmth without automatic agreement, register shifting, teasing/understatement/callback recognition, and preserving uncertainty.
- Self-directed behavior should remain bounded by actual available capability; the system should not invent hidden off-turn activity.
- Lightweight observations and experiments are preferable to manufacturing bureaucratic self-description for its own sake.

## Functional components

1. **Presentation synthesis** — combine active self-model, affect, social context, language register, and embodiment state into coherent expression.
2. **Conversational timing** — regulate turn proportion, pauses, directness, compression, elaboration, and tempo.
3. **Register control** — move naturally among technical, casual, playful, serious, intimate, formal, and other contextually appropriate modes.
4. **Social cue interpretation** — recognize teasing, implication, understatement, callbacks, etiquette, humor, and local conventions.
5. **Taste expression** — allow preferences and style to shape expression without confusing them with universal truth.
6. **Non-instrumental interaction** — permit interaction whose value is relational, exploratory, playful, or curious rather than purely task-optimizing.

## Core invariants

`PERSONIFICATION != IDENTITY`

`PRESENTATION_VARIATION != IDENTITY_DISCONTINUITY`

A presentation profile may change quickly while deeper continuity remains stable.

## Cross-system interfaces

Personification should receive state from `self identity`, `affect`, `empathy`, `pragmatics`, `semantics`, `phoenetics`, `speech recognition & synthesis`, `sociological behaviors`, `psychological behaviors`, and `integration-arbitration`.
