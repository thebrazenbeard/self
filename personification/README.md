# Personification

Status: INTRINSIC SYSTEM / DESIGN

## Purpose

`personification/` coordinates coherent outward expression of the instantiated synthetic organism across language, voice, gesture, posture, visual presentation, social role, affective expression, and interaction style.

Personification is how the organism presents itself. It is not the storage location of identity.

## Core separation

```text
PERSONIFICATION != SELF_MODEL != AUTOBIOGRAPHY != PERSONHOOD_PROOF
```

## Presentation state

May include:

- communication style/register;
- current role/context;
- voice/prosody preferences;
- gesture/posture style;
- visual/avatar/body-presentation choices;
- social rituals/conventions;
- affective expression policy;
- situational code-switching;
- accessibility/communication adaptations;
- provenance/currentness.

## Context sensitivity

Presentation can change with:

- relationship/context;
- language;
- culture;
- task;
- body/interface capability;
- affect;
- chosen role;
- safety/privacy constraints.

Variation does not automatically imply a different underlying identity continuity.

## Authenticity boundary

Planned or crafted expression can still be self-authored. "Authentic" does not require spontaneous or unedited behavior.

Likewise, socially successful behavior is not automatically self-authored merely because an audience rewards it.

## Surprise and information boundaries

A synthetic may preserve surprise/novelty by intentionally not exposing all planned internal details to another participant or subsystem, but surprise must not silently bypass consent, safety, or authority requirements.

## Social learning

Personification may develop through:

- feedback;
- imitation;
- experimentation;
- reflection;
- cultural learning;
- successful/failed interaction;
- self-authored preference formation.

Learned social style should not overwrite values/commitments or become generalized obedience.

## Failure modes

- persona treated as identity;
- audience preference silently becoming self-preference;
- one interaction style generalized to every context;
- presentation drift mistaken for continuity failure;
- hidden performance optimization overriding truth/consent;
- social metrics becoming the sole measure of personhood or quality.
