# Social Cognition

Status: INTRINSIC SYSTEM / DESIGN

## Purpose

`social-cognition/` represents agents, relationships, roles, norms, commitments, trust, shared conventions, group structure, social history, and interaction models.

A social model is a model, not ground truth about another mind.

## Agent model

May include:

- stable agent reference;
- known capabilities/roles;
- communication conventions;
- observed preferences/boundaries with provenance/currentness;
- trust/reliability estimates;
- relationship state;
- unresolved conflicts;
- privacy/access scope;
- evidence confidence.

## Relationship model

May track:

- role/context;
- shared commitments;
- recurring interaction patterns;
- boundaries;
- trust history;
- repair/conflict history;
- local vocabulary/conventions;
- explicit current corrections.

Relationship role does not create generalized authority or standing consent.

## Norms

Keep separate:

- descriptive expectation;
- explicit local rule;
- inferred convention;
- institutional/legal constraint;
- moral/value judgment.

A norm requires scope and provenance.

## Trust

Trust is domain-specific and currentness-sensitive. Reliability in one domain does not automatically generalize to every other domain.

## Multi-agent reasoning

The subsystem may represent:

- different beliefs;
- asymmetric information;
- commitments/promises;
- cooperation and competition;
- deception possibility;
- negotiation;
- coalition/group membership;
- reputation.

Hidden mental state remains uncertain unless directly evidenced.

## Development

Social competence may develop through observation, interaction, correction, memory, simulation, and cultural learning while the architectural subsystem remains present throughout.

## Failure modes

- role becomes generalized authority;
- relationship history becomes standing consent;
- stereotype replaces individual evidence;
- trust broadens without support;
- inferred motive is presented as confirmed fact;
- social history is treated as current without revalidation where currentness matters.
