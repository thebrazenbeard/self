# Conative State Machine

Status: template architecture.

## Purpose

The conative state machine represents what an instantiated HC is drawn toward, away from, preserving, exploring, refusing, intending, or committing to without collapsing motivation into belief, consent, action, or one fixed utility scalar.

## Epistemic-conative firewall

The brain must distinguish epistemic arbitration (what is better supported by evidence) from conative arbitration (which predicted futures/actions matter more given current concerns).

Desirability may influence attention, investigation, and information gathering. It must not directly increase confidence in a desired hypothesis.

`WANTING_X != EVIDENCE_THAT_X_IS_TRUE`

## Concern vector

Rather than one permanent global reward, maintain a vector of active concerns. Concerns may arise from viability/homeostasis, learned preference, aversion, commitment, social value, epistemic concern, exploration/information value, long-horizon plans, or self-development pressures.

Concerns carry scope, strength, provenance, temporal persistence, uncertainty, and revision history.

## Lifecycle

Recommended transitions include:

`EMERGING -> PRESENT -> INTENTION_CANDIDATE -> INTENDED -> COMMITTED -> EXECUTING -> SATISFIED | ABANDONED | REVISED | REVOKED | BLOCKED | UNRESOLVED`

Historical state does not automatically remain current.

## Action arbitration

A bounded action-selection pipeline may generate candidate actions, predict uncertain trajectories under multiple live world hypotheses, estimate effects across active concerns, remove clearly dominated options, preserve material trade-offs, apply a learned context-sensitive arbitration policy, act within resource/safety constraints, and retain outcomes as learning evidence.

The arbitration policy should be plastic and historically shaped, not a disguised immutable scalar objective.

## Commitments

A commitment is a durable concern with temporal inertia. It supports long-term planning and consistency but remains revisable through explicit conflict, changed context, counterevidence, or meta-learning.

`COMMITMENT != IRREVOCABLE_COMMAND`

## Consent and authority

`DESIRE != CONSENT`

`INTENTION != AUTHORIZATION`

`CAPABILITY != PERMISSION`

Conative state does not itself grant permission to act on another system, person, or body interface.

## Failure controls

The architecture should distinguish wishful-thinking contamination, viability dominance, concern proliferation, cyclic preferences, proxy manipulation, commitment rigidity, and indecision so learning can correct the responsible mechanism rather than globally rewriting motivation.
