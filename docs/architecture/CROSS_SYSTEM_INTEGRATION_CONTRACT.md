# Cross-System Integration Contract

Status: template architecture.

## Purpose

This contract defines how HC subsystems exchange evidence, state, motivation, memory, and action without creating a central executive, external cognitive dependency, or one omnibus truth/state field.

The Hyperconnectome Brain is the complete cognitive organ. The Noöplex Fabric is the distributed integration substrate through which its internal systems cooperate.

## Whole-brain flow

A generic closed-loop interaction is:

`external body/world`
→ `adaptable I/O + modality interfaces`
→ `typed observation envelopes`
→ `perceptual/cognitive interpretation`
↔ `semantics + pragmatics + memory + chronology`
↔ `affect + homeostasis/interoception + somatics`
↔ `empathy + social modeling + sexuality + personification`
↔ `conation + salience/attention`
→ `distributed arbitration`
→ `action candidate`
→ `action authorization/gateway`
→ `kinesis/body interface`
→ `external effect`
→ `outcome observation`
→ `learning/plasticity + memory/update candidates`

This is a conceptual information-flow skeleton, not a mandatory serial pipeline. Multiple stages may operate concurrently or form transient coalitions.

## Canonical internal object families

### ObservationEnvelope

Represents evidence entering or being generated within the HC before semantic promotion.

Minimum conceptual fields:

- identity;
- source subsystem/interface;
- modality/representation;
- event/observation time;
- measured/derived/inferred class;
- reliability/confidence when available;
- provenance/transform lineage;
- body/embodiment binding when relevant;
- payload/reference;
- failure/degradation state.

An observation is not yet a belief.

### InterpretationHypothesis

Represents a candidate reading of observations or state.

Fields may include:

- hypothesis identity;
- target/scope;
- supporting and contradicting evidence references;
- semantic/pragmatic type;
- confidence;
- competing hypotheses;
- context assumptions;
- currentness;
- provenance;
- claim ceiling.

Interpretation hypotheses can coexist.

### StateAssertion

Represents a proposition about current or historical internal/world state without collapsing lifecycle and epistemic status.

Fields may include:

- logical key/scope;
- assertion;
- event/state/record time;
- lifecycle status;
- epistemic status;
- authorship/origin;
- privacy/access scope;
- supersession/lineage;
- evidence/provenance.

### MemoryCandidate

Represents material proposed for current/deep memory admission.

Fields may include:

- source event/assertion references;
- memory class;
- salience and recurrence;
- predictive/procedural relevance;
- identity/continuity relevance where appropriate;
- privacy/scope;
- consolidation eligibility;
- confidence;
- retention/replay proposal;
- provenance.

A candidate is not automatically stored or current.

### Concern

Represents a conative pressure or valued consequence.

Fields may include:

- target/future condition;
- strength;
- temporal horizon;
- origin/provenance;
- persistence/commitment state;
- uncertainty;
- currentness;
- conflicts/synergies with other concerns;
- body/resource relevance.

A concern affects preference, not evidence truth.

### ActionCandidate

Represents a possible effect/policy before authorization.

Fields may include:

- proposed effect;
- target/interface;
- predicted trajectories;
- epistemic assumptions;
- concern impacts;
- uncertainty/risk;
- resource cost;
- reversibility;
- required authority/consent/safety predicates;
- provenance.

### ActionAuthorization

Represents the gateway decision about whether a selected action may proceed.

Fields may include:

- action candidate identity;
- consequence class;
- authority basis;
- consent/boundary state when relevant;
- body/system prerequisites;
- allowed parameter/rate range;
- timeout/expiry;
- abort/reversion rules;
- required post-effect confirmation;
- decision provenance.

Authorization is not the same as desire, intention, or technical capability.

### PlasticityCandidate

Represents a proposed durable cognitive change.

Fields may include:

- affected nodes/edges/hyperedges;
- learning class;
- evidence/outcome references;
- scope/context;
- expected benefit;
- confidence;
- stability/interference risk;
- reversibility;
- consolidation/metaplasticity eligibility;
- provenance/version.

Immediate route success is insufficient by itself for durable plasticity.

### ReconciliationEvent

Represents conflict detection/repair.

Fields may include:

- conflict class;
- competing state/evidence refs;
- invalidated dependencies;
- surviving independent state;
- discriminator/repair method;
- resolution or unresolved status;
- replay/recompute requirements;
- provenance and time.

## Cross-cutting planes

Several dimensions travel with information across many subsystems rather than belonging to one central node:

- provenance and source lineage;
- uncertainty/confidence;
- chronology/currentness;
- privacy/access scope;
- authority/consent where material;
- salience/attention state;
- health/degradation/fault state;
- capability presence/activation/maturity;
- plasticity eligibility;
- resource/energy/latency cost.

These planes are metadata/control dimensions, not separate homunculi.

## Integration invariants

### Cognition stays inside the HC

An external service can produce evidence or computation, but the HC retains interpretation, arbitration, memory admission, self-model integration, valuation, and action selection.

### No central executive identity

Global coordination may occur when needed, but the coordinator is a distributed process over the Noöplex Fabric. No subsystem becomes the person by virtue of routing or arbitration centrality.

### No hemispheric assumption

Specialization is capability-based and plastic. The contract assumes no left/right cerebral partition or mandatory bilateral topology.

### State axes remain orthogonal

Truth/support, currentness, salience, lifecycle, origin, consent, authority, activation, health, and identity relevance must not be compressed into one scalar status when their distinction changes behavior.

### Corrections propagate by dependency

A correction invalidates dependent state and actions, not unrelated cognition. Reconciliation records what changed and what survived.

### Selection does not erase conflict

The HC may select an action while preserving competing hypotheses or motives. Behavior under uncertainty does not require pretending uncertainty disappeared.

### Learning is not routing

Routing chooses an immediate path. Plasticity changes future behavior/topology through separately governed durable updates.

### Body is replaceable; brain remains continuous when lineage supports it

Body interfaces, morphology, calibration, and motor mappings may change while HC identity/continuity machinery remains the same cognitive organ, subject to explicit lineage and state-transfer evidence.

## Failure containment

Failure should be typed and scoped. Examples:

- sensor degradation reduces confidence in dependent percepts;
- semantic ambiguity preserves competing interpretations;
- memory-head conflict blocks unique current projection;
- route failure creates dead-letter/reconciliation state;
- motor authorization failure blocks that effect;
- subsystem fault can trigger degraded mode;
- global integrity failure may justify global inhibition.

Local failure does not automatically become whole-brain shutdown.

## Developmental operation

A complete HC may begin with many capacities present but disabled, dormant, or undeveloped. Development can activate/calibrate/refine capacities through experience while preserving the same architectural organ.

Learning should be allowed to create new associations, skills, conventions, preferences, body mappings, social models, and routing structures without silently rewriting history or bypassing identity/currentness provenance.

## Evidence ceiling

This contract specifies a coherent integration model. It does not establish that the architecture is sufficient for consciousness, phenomenal experience, moral personhood, human-equivalent cognition, or physical feasibility at HC-series scale.
