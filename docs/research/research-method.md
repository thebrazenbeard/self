# HC-1R Research Method

## Objective

Develop a theoretically possible, internally coherent, falsifiable, and progressively testable concept for a Noöplex hyperconnectome brain without claiming that the full system exists or that any proposed measurement verifies phenomenal consciousness.

## Unit of argument

Every substantive design decision should be expressible as:

1. **Problem:** the cognitive, physical, or assurance function required.
2. **Candidate mechanism:** the proposed architectural response.
3. **Evidence:** direct source support for the mechanism or a close component.
4. **Integration inference:** why the mechanism might work in HC-1R.
5. **Counterevidence or limitation:** what the source does not establish.
6. **Observable:** what a model or future prototype could measure.
7. **Falsifier:** what result would make the candidate mechanism fail or require redesign.
8. **Classification:** demonstrated, extrapolated, speculative, or rejected.

## Evidence priority

Prefer:

1. peer-reviewed primary research;
2. peer-reviewed technical reviews when a field is broad or contested;
3. official manufacturer or laboratory technical documentation;
4. standards, government technical reports, and benchmark specifications;
5. preprints for emerging work, labeled as such;
6. secondary explainers only for discovery, never as the sole support for a material claim.

Fiction, marketing, blogs, and conversational summaries may motivate a question. They do not establish feasibility.

## Hostile review questions

For every attractive idea, ask:

- Does the evidence demonstrate the claimed function, or only something adjacent?
- Is the mechanism useful, or merely biologically recognizable?
- What scale was demonstrated?
- What disappears when device noise, communication cost, calibration, latency, and thermal limits are included?
- Does a behavioral result distinguish the proposed mechanism from a simpler alternative?
- Does the concept create observability or safety failures?
- Is a consciousness claim being smuggled into a functional engineering result?
- Can the idea be removed without changing the architecture's testable behavior?

## Classification discipline

### DEMONSTRATED

Use only when a cited system or experiment directly demonstrates the relevant mechanism. Scope must be stated. A demonstrated memristive reservoir, for example, does not demonstrate a conscious hyperconnectome.

### EXTRAPOLATED

Use when all major ingredients exist but their combination, scale, reliability, or role in cognition has not been demonstrated.

### SPECULATIVE

Use when a key mechanism, interpretation, or scale is unsupported, presently untestable, or dependent on unresolved science.

### REJECTED

Use when the proposal relies on a false premise, supplies no defined workload, violates known physical constraints, or adds complexity without a measurable function.

## Research expansion strategy

Outside-the-box searches are required, but novelty is not a selection criterion. Candidate ideas are drawn from:

- neuroscience and connectomics;
- cognitive architecture and consciousness science;
- dynamical systems and complex networks;
- control theory and cybernetics;
- distributed systems and runtime assurance;
- robotics, morphology, and developmental learning;
- analog, neuromorphic, photonic, spintronic, and biological computing;
- immunology-inspired anomaly detection and self-maintenance;
- memory consolidation and offline replay;
- compiler, network-on-chip, and heterogeneous-system design.

Each candidate must survive the unit-of-argument test before entering HC-1R.

## Consciousness boundary

The project may evaluate theory-derived indicator properties such as recurrence, selective broadcast, metacognitive monitoring, embodiment, causal differentiation, and perturbational response. These are architectural or behavioral properties. Satisfying them would not prove subjective experience.

The project keeps separate:

- **functional consciousness-like capacities** — reportability, global availability, self-monitoring, flexible control;
- **theory-derived indicators** — properties motivated by IIT, GNWT, RPT, HOT, predictive processing, or related theories;
- **phenomenal consciousness** — subjective experience, which is not operationally verified by this architecture;
- **moral status** — a governance and ethics question that cannot be reduced to a benchmark score.

## Change policy

- Research work occurs on a clean branch.
- Source provenance is preserved.
- Proposed redesigns are never backfilled into historical lineage.
- Claims are revised by appending corrections or changing the current proposal with a documented reason.
- No merge, deployment, force-push, fabrication, model training, wetware work, or protected effect is implied by a research commit.
