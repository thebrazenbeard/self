# Primary-Source Register

This is a living research register, not a bibliography-as-decoration. Each entry states what the source supports and what it does **not** establish for HC-1R.

## Network and dynamical organization

| ID | Source | Relevant support | Boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| NET-01 | Bullmore & Sporns, [Complex brain networks](https://www.nature.com/articles/nrn2575) (2009) | Biological brain networks exhibit modularity, hubs, hierarchy, and small-world properties while balancing communication efficiency and wiring cost | Does not show that copying these graph statistics produces cognition or consciousness |
| NET-02 | Bullmore & Sporns, [The economy of brain network organization](https://www.nature.com/articles/nrn3214) (2012) | Connectomes trade topological efficiency and robustness against expensive long-range connections; connector hubs and rich clubs matter | Does not justify maximizing connectivity or hub degree |
| NET-03 | Boccaletti et al., [The structure and dynamics of multilayer networks](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7332224/) (2014) | Formal basis for systems whose components interact through several relationship layers | A multilayer description is a model; physical layer realization remains an engineering question |
| NET-04 | Holme & Saramäki, [Temporal Networks](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.physrep.2012.03.001) (2012) | Edge activation time and ordering can change reachability and dynamics independently of the static graph | Does not prescribe HC-1R's time constants or routing policy |
| NET-05 | Battiston et al., [The physics of higher-order interactions in complex systems](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41567-021-01371-4) (2021) | Hypergraphs and simplicial complexes can represent interactions involving groups rather than pairs | A useful higher-order model does not prove literal physical many-body connections are required |
| NET-06 | Gu et al., [Controllability of structural brain networks](https://www.nature.com/articles/ncomms9414) (2015) | Shows how network-control metrics can relate topology to state transitions in a simplified linear brain-network model | Authors explicitly note that the link to cognitive control remains incomplete; nonlinear HC-1R dynamics require separate validation |
| NET-07 | Khona & Fiete, [Attractor and integrator networks in the brain](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41583-022-00642-0) (2022) | Attractor dynamics can maintain working state, integrate noisy evidence, and correct errors | Attractors can also become rigid, spurious, or pathological; they do not establish subjective experience |
| NET-08 | La Camera et al., [Metastable dynamics of neural circuits and networks](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8900181/) (2022) | Transient occupancy of metastable states is associated with perception, expectation, attention, and decisions | Association is not a unique causal explanation; “metastability” must be operationally measured |
| NET-09 | Hahn et al., [Portraits of communication in neuronal networks](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41583-018-0094-0) (2019) | Reviews routing through synchrony, resonance, and coherence between neuronal populations | Oscillatory coherence is one routing mechanism, not a universal binding solution |
| NET-10 | O'Byrne & Jerbi, [Metastability demystified](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41583-024-00883-1) (2024) | Clarifies competing definitions and measurements of metastability | Prevents using the word as an unmeasured synonym for flexible cognition |
| NET-11 | Zeraati et al., [Theoretical foundations of studying criticality in the brain](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11117095/) (2024) | Provides a taxonomy of critical, quasi-critical, and self-organized regimes and documents continuing controversy | “Edge of chaos” is not accepted as a mandatory brain or cognition property |
| NET-12 | Hoel et al., [Quantifying causal emergence shows that macro can beat micro](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3856819/) (2013) | Provides a formal way to ask whether a macro-description carries more effective information than a micro-description | Requires a specified causal model and interventions; does not prove consciousness or ontological emergence |

## Consciousness and cognitive organization

| ID | Source | Relevant support | Boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| COG-01 | Albantakis et al., [Integrated Information Theory 4.0](https://journals.plos.org/ploscompbiol/article?id=10.1371/journal.pcbi.1011465) (2023) | Defines IIT in terms of intrinsic cause-effect structure and irreducibility | IIT is not a raw connectivity threshold; exact large-system evaluation is generally intractable and the theory remains contested |
| COG-02 | Melloni et al., [Adversarial testing of global neuronal workspace and integrated information theories](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-025-08888-1) (2025) | Large preregistered multimodal experiment directly tested contrasting predictions | Results challenged important predictions of both theories and did not select a final winner |
| COG-03 | Del Pin et al., [The science of consciousness after an adversarial collaboration](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/science/articles/10.3389/fsci.2025.1546279/full) (2025) | Reviews theory plurality, measurement limits, Φ intractability, and the distinction between IIT-inspired measures and Φ | No current measure provides a consciousness certificate |
| COG-04 | Mashour et al., [Conscious Processing and the Global Neuronal Workspace Hypothesis](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8770991/) (2020) | GNW proposes recurrent ignition, amplification, sustained representation, and global access by specialized processors | Global broadcast may explain access and reportability without settling phenomenology |
| COG-05 | Dehaene, Kerszberg & Changeux, [A neuronal model of a global workspace](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC24407/) (1998) | Formal distributed architecture with specialized processors and long-distance workspace connectivity | A model architecture is not proof that the same layout is necessary or sufficient for consciousness |
| COG-06 | Butlin et al., [Consciousness in Artificial Intelligence](https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.08708) (2023 preprint) | Derives computational indicator properties from recurrent processing, workspace, higher-order, predictive, attention-schema, agency, and embodiment theories | Satisfying indicators would raise evidential questions; it would not prove an AI is conscious |
| COG-07 | Casali et al., [A theoretically based index of consciousness independent of sensory processing and behavior](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23946194/) (2013) | Introduces perturbational complexity for human brain states | PCI depends on human neurophysiology and is not Φ or a validated machine-consciousness measure |
| COG-08 | Graziano & Webb, [The attention schema theory](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4407481/) (2015) | Motivates an internal simplified model of attention for attention control and awareness reports | Can explain self-reports without establishing subjective experience; should be tested as a control model |
| COG-09 | Friston, [The free-energy principle](https://www.nature.com/articles/nrn2787) (2010) | Relates perception, learning, gain, and action to generative models and free-energy minimization | A broad normative account is not a unique implementable algorithm and should not replace established control methods without comparative evidence |
| COG-10 | Halassa & Kastner, [Thalamic functions in distributed cognitive control](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41593-017-0020-1) (2017) | Thalamic circuits can shift and sustain functional interactions among spatially separated cortical computations | Inspires dynamic routing/gain control, not a requirement to reproduce thalamic anatomy |
| COG-11 | Tanaka, Ishikawa, Lee & Kakei, [The cerebro-cerebellum as a locus of forward model](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7160920/) (2020) | Reviews evidence for predictive forward models in motor and higher functions | Does not imply one universal cerebellar algorithm or validate a direct artificial analogue |
| COG-12 | Park, Coddington & Dudman, [Basal Ganglia Circuits for Action Specification](https://www.annualreviews.org/content/journals/10.1146/annurev-neuro-070918-050452) (2020) | Supports dedicated selection, reinforcement, and graded action-control functions | Biological mapping is contested and should motivate interfaces rather than anatomical imitation |

## Learning, memory, regulation, and embodiment

| ID | Source | Relevant support | Boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| MEM-01 | McClelland, McNaughton & O'Reilly, [Why there are complementary learning systems](https://doi.org/10.1037/0033-295X.102.3.419) (1995) | Motivates fast episodic acquisition separated from slower statistical integration to reduce interference | HC-1R still needs a tested machine implementation and explicit provenance semantics |
| MEM-02 | Diekelmann & Born, [The memory function of sleep](https://www.nature.com/articles/nrn2762) (2010) | Supports offline replay, redistribution, reorganization, and synaptic renormalization as memory functions | Does not require a synthetic system to imitate biological sleep stages or glymphatic claims |
| MEM-03 | Turrigiano & Nelson, [Homeostatic plasticity in the developing nervous system](https://www.nature.com/articles/nrn1327) (2004) | Adaptive networks require negative-feedback mechanisms to prevent runaway excitation or quiescence | Artificial setpoints and stability laws require independent derivation and testing |
| MEM-04 | Holtmaat & Svoboda, [Experience-dependent structural synaptic plasticity](https://www.nature.com/articles/nrn2699) (2009) | Adult biological circuits change connection structure as well as synaptic strength | Physical rewiring in HC-1R is optional; virtual topology changes may provide the testable functional analogue |
| MEM-05 | Gerstner et al., [Eligibility traces and three-factor learning rules](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/neural-circuits/articles/10.3389/fncir.2018.00053/full) (2018) | Supports local co-activity traces gated by delayed modulatory factors for temporal credit assignment | Does not establish one global dopamine-like scalar or solve all long-horizon credit assignment |
| MEM-06 | Barrett & Simmons, [Interoceptive predictions in the brain](https://www.nature.com/articles/nrn3950) (2015) | Motivates predictive regulation of internal body state and allostasis | A synthetic interoceptive model should use actual machine state, not pretend-biological variables |
| EMB-01 | Pfeifer, Iida & Gómez, [Morphological computation for adaptive behavior and cognition](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ics.2005.12.080) (2006) | Body morphology and environment can perform part of the control computation | Does not mean passive mechanics replace cognition or assurance |
| EMB-02 | Ciria et al., [Predictive Processing in Cognitive Robotics](https://arxiv.org/abs/2101.06611) (2021 preprint) | Reviews implemented predictive-processing ideas and major gaps in multimodal integration, control, and precision weighting | Documents that many robotics implementations remain partial and terminology is often conflated |
| EMB-03 | Open X-Embodiment Collaboration, [Open X-Embodiment](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.08864) (2023) | Demonstrates cross-robot embodied datasets and generalist policy research | Dataset transfer does not create a persistent self-model or solve safe continual learning |
| EMB-04 | NVIDIA, [Isaac Lab](https://developer.nvidia.com/isaac/lab) | Demonstrates an available simulation framework for robot learning and sim-to-real research | Product availability does not validate HC-1R's learning architecture |

## Neuromorphic and unconventional substrates

| ID | Source | Relevant support | Boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| HW-01 | Intel, [Hala Point](https://newsroom.intel.com/artificial-intelligence/intel-builds-worlds-largest-neuromorphic-system-to-enable-more-sustainable-ai) (2024) | Demonstrates a large event-driven Loihi 2 research system: 1.15 billion modeled neurons and 128 billion synapses | Modeled-neuron counts are not cognitive capacity; system consumes about 2.6 kW and is not a humanoid brain |
| HW-02 | IBM, [HERMES mixed-signal in-memory compute chip](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41928-023-01010-1) (2023) | Demonstrates 64 analog PCM compute cores with digital functions and on-chip communication | Demonstrates DNN inference acceleration, not a large asynchronous spiking hyperconnectome |
| HW-03 | Höppner et al., [SpiNNaker2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.04491) (2024 preprint) | Demonstrates heterogeneous many-core design combining ARM processors, accelerators, event routing, and external memory | Does not establish HC-1R scale, cognition, or consciousness |
| HW-04 | Lava project, [Lava Architecture](https://lava-nc.org/lava_architecture_overview.html) | Demonstrates programmable, composable, cross-platform processes and refinement from abstract models to neuromorphic backends | Refutes the claim that neuromorphic cognition must be software-free; framework remains evolving |
| HW-05 | NIR authors, [Neuromorphic Intermediate Representation](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-024-52259-9) (2024) | Demonstrates a portable graph representation across multiple neuromorphic platforms | Portability remains limited by backend semantics and device differences |
| HW-06 | Furber et al., [Neuromorphic computing at scale](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-024-08253-8) (2024) | Reviews scaling requirements, ecosystem gaps, applications, and research directions | Scaling hardware alone does not identify the algorithms needed for general cognition |
| HW-07 | Mehonic et al., [The road to commercial success for neuromorphic technologies](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-025-57352-1) (2025) | Documents device drift, mismatch, write costs, reliability, and application constraints | Undercuts assumptions that analog/mixed-signal substrates are mature or automatically efficient |
| HW-08 | Milano et al., [In-materia reservoir computing with self-organizing nanowire networks](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41563-021-01099-9) (2022) | Demonstrates physical recurrent dynamics and fading memory in a designless memristive nanowire reservoir | Readout/training and task scale are limited; self-organization is not synonymous with self-directed cognition |
| HW-09 | Kuncic et al., [Self-organization of inhomogeneous memristive hardware](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-022-33476-6) (2022) | Demonstrates online structural and weight adaptation in a memristive spiking recurrent architecture | Component experiment does not establish safe open-ended structural plasticity |
| HW-10 | Shastri et al., [Integrated photonic neuromorphic computing](https://www.nature.com/articles/s44287-024-00050-9) (2024) | Reviews high-throughput optical linear operations, multiplexing, and photonic-neuromorphic integration paths | Optical I/O, nonlinearity, memory, precision, and total system energy can erase headline advantages |
| HW-11 | Grollier et al., [Neuromorphic computing with spintronics](https://www.nature.com/articles/s44306-024-00019-2) (2024) | Reviews spintronic synapses, oscillators, networks, and physical reservoirs | Field remains early; many demonstrations require conventional post-processing |
| HW-12 | Cai et al., [Brain organoid reservoir computing](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41928-023-01069-w) (2023) | Demonstrates organoid-based reservoir dynamics for speech recognition and nonlinear prediction | Does not demonstrate consciousness, general cognition, reliable scaling, or ethical acceptability |
| HW-13 | Kagan et al., [DishBrain](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neuron.2022.09.001) (2022) | Demonstrates closed-loop task adaptation in cultured human and rodent cortical neurons | Cultured neurons are not a brain organoid; performance does not prove sentience despite the paper's title |

## Assurance, safety, and evaluation

| ID | Source | Relevant support | Boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| SAFE-01 | NASA, [Formal Verification Framework for Runtime Assurance](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/20240006522) (2024) | Formalizes Simplex-style switching from an untrusted advanced controller to a trusted safety controller | Requires valid safety envelopes and a capable fallback; cannot guarantee arbitrary cognition is safe |
| SAFE-02 | NASA Armstrong, [Resilient Autonomy and Multi-Monitor Runtime Assurance](https://www.nasa.gov/centers-and-facilities/armstrong/autonomous-systems/) | Demonstrates an assurance direction that binds untrusted autonomy using independent monitors | Operational evidence is domain-specific and does not validate synthetic-humanoid autonomy |
| SAFE-03 | NIST, [Autonomous Systems Assurance](https://www.nist.gov/programs-projects/autonomous-systems-assurance) | Establishes input-space coverage and combinatorial testing as open assurance problems | Coverage cannot prove correct behavior over an unbounded environment |
| SAFE-04 | NASA, [R2U2 runtime verification and system-health management](https://www.nasa.gov/directorates/stmd/space-tech-research-grants/multi-platform-multi-architecture-runtime-verification-of-autonomous-space-systems/) | Supports real-time monitoring with timing guarantees and low interference | Monitors are only as good as the properties, sensors, and failure models supplied |
| SAFE-05 | NeuroBench, [A framework for benchmarking neuromorphic computing algorithms and systems](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-025-56739-4) (2025) | Provides task, accuracy, latency, energy, and system-measurement discipline | Existing benchmarks do not cover persistent identity, open-world cognition, or consciousness |

## Quantum boundary

| ID | Source | Relevant support | Boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| Q-01 | Tang, [A quantum-inspired classical algorithm for recommendation systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/1807.04271) (2018) | Demonstrates that some apparent quantum machine-learning advantages can be classically dequantized under comparable data assumptions | Does not show all quantum ML speedups are impossible |
| Q-02 | Huang et al., [Power of data in quantum machine learning](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-021-22539-9) (2021) | Establishes data-access and sample-complexity constraints on quantum learning advantages | Supports workload-specific analysis, not generic acceleration |
| Q-03 | Bardin, Slichter & Reilly, [Microwaves in Quantum Computing](https://www.nist.gov/publications/microwaves-quantum-computing) (2021) | Reviews control, readout, and microwave-engineering constraints across leading qubit platforms | Reinforces that useful quantum hardware requires a specialized control and environmental stack, not a generic warm on-head coprocessor |
| Q-04 | Song et al., [High-density wiring solution for 500-qubit scale superconducting quantum processors](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41081328/) (2025) | Demonstrates a high-density control-and-measurement wiring approach at the 500-qubit scale | A wiring demonstration does not establish fault-tolerant computation or a cognition advantage |

## Source-handling rule

The register establishes component evidence. HC-1R's system-level feasibility remains **EXTRAPOLATED** until its interfaces, scale, power, failure behavior, and evaluation results are jointly demonstrated.
