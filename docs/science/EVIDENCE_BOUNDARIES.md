# Evidence Boundaries and Scientific Grounding

This repository is speculative engineering. Every design claim should be understood in one of three classes.

## DOCUMENTED PRESENT-DAY SCIENCE

Current literature supports the following building blocks individually:

- brain organoids and biohybrid neural interfaces as research platforms;
- neuromorphic hardware and memristor-based accelerators;
- integrated nonlinear/quantum photonics;
- photonic quantum-computing modules, with many components operating at or near room temperature even when some sources/detectors require specialized conditions;
- nitrogen-vacancy centers in diamond for quantum sensing over wide temperature ranges;
- hypothalamic, endocrine, autonomic, interoceptive and neuromodulatory contributions to human affect and homeostasis.

## ENGINEERING EXTRAPOLATION

Not presently demonstrated as a complete system:

- an adult human-equivalent synthetic encephalon assembled from engineered neuroglial tissue;
- whole-brain-scale high-density bidirectional neuroelectronic interfacing;
- durable vascularized synthetic neural tissue at human-brain scale;
- seamless high-bandwidth coupling of such tissue to embedded photonic/quantum accelerators;
- a fully synthetic endocrine system reproducing human affective physiology with arbitrary programmability;
- consciousness transfer into such a substrate.

These should be described as design targets, hypotheses, or canon assumptions—not current engineering capability.

## CANON ASSUMPTIONS

The Wreckforge Synthetic architecture may assume technologies beyond current demonstration. When that happens, the documentation should identify the extrapolation explicitly rather than disguising it as established science.

## Corrections to earlier concept drafts

A few tempting but scientifically weak claims are rejected here:

- Quantum computers do not provide generic "exponential parallelism" that can simply accelerate arbitrary cognition.
- Coherent Ising machines and photonic analog processors are not automatically universal quantum computers.
- NV centers are excellent quantum sensors; that does not establish whole-brain noninvasive neuron-by-neuron readout.
- More synapses do not monotonically imply greater intelligence.
- Human emotions cannot be reduced to one hormone per emotion.
- Testosterone is not an "anger hormone," oxytocin is not simply a "trust hormone," and cortisol is not simply a "stress chemical"; effects are receptor-, region-, dose-, history-, sex-, and context-dependent.
- A quantum subsystem should be treated as a co-processor unless the fictional setting explicitly establishes something beyond known physics.
