# Runtime Invariants

Status: template operating contract.

## Purpose

These invariants constrain how HC subsystems interact at runtime. They are cross-cutting rules, not a central executive process.

## Cognitive-organ boundary

`NO_ESSENTIAL_COGNITION_OUTSIDE_HC`

External sensors, actuators, databases, model services, and computational peripherals may contribute signals/results, but interpretation, learning, memory, valuation, conation, identity continuity, and action selection remain HC-owned.

## Topology

`NO_HEMISPHERIC_DECOMPOSITION`

`NO_HOMUNCULAR_EXECUTIVE`

Subsystem folders are ownership/packaging boundaries; runtime cognition is distributed through the Noöplex Fabric.

## State separation

`OBSERVATION != INTERPRETATION`

`MEASURED != DERIVED != INFERRED`

`INTERPRETATION != EVIDENCE`

`PREDICTION != FACT`

`SALIENT != TRUE`

`RECENT != CURRENT`

`STORED != CURRENT`

`ROUTED != INCORPORATED`

`DELIVERED != BELIEVED`

`PRIORITY != AUTHORITY`

`DESIRE != CONSENT`

`DESIRE != BELIEF`

`INTENTION != AUTHORIZATION`

`CAPABILITY != PERMISSION`

`ACTION_SELECTED != ACTION_AUTHORIZED`

`COMMAND_SENT != EFFECT_CONFIRMED`

`PRESENTATION != IDENTITY`

`SELF_MODEL != WHOLE_INSTANTIATED_MIND`

## Presence and activation

Capability presence, activation, maturity, health, and authorization are independent axes. A subsystem may exist while disabled, dormant, developing, inhibited, degraded, or faulted.

`DISABLED != DELETED`

`DORMANT != FORGOTTEN`

## Currentness and history

Historical evidence should be append-oriented. Correction, supersession, revocation, contradiction, and reinterpretation add lineage rather than rewriting past records.

A unique current projection may only be produced when the relevant current-state contract supports it. Competing heads remain explicit.

## Epistemic/conative firewall

Desirability may change attention and information gathering. It must not directly raise or lower evidence confidence.

World-model confidence is updated through evidence-bearing processes; action preference is resolved through conative arbitration.

## Routing and authority

Routing decides where processing occurs. It does not grant action authority or semantic truth.

Every materially consequential effect should retain an authority/consequence boundary independent of routing success.

## Plasticity

Immediate activation or repeated route use does not automatically become durable plastic change. Durable learning requires the appropriate evidence, scope, consolidation/metaplasticity, and provenance.

Temporary modes must not silently become identity or global policy.

## Failure visibility

Unroutable, contradictory, expired, schema-incompatible, faulted, or otherwise unresolved state should be preserved through explicit unresolved/dead-letter/reconciliation mechanisms rather than silently discarded.

Local failure should remain local unless the consequence genuinely requires whole-brain inhibition.

## Embodiment

Body configuration, sensor/actuator calibration, body schema, and motor policy are separable from brain identity.

A body change may require relearning and revalidation without implying identity replacement.

## Provenance/currentness

Every subsystem should be able to distinguish source evidence, derived state, current projections, historical state, and action effects when those distinctions materially affect reasoning or behavior.

## Evidence ceiling

A subsystem may only claim what its evidence and validation support. Engineering convenience, file placement, component naming, or successful task completion do not by themselves prove cognition, semantics, causation, consciousness, personhood, or universal competence.
